# Carnivores 2 MEE v1.1.9.2 Beta (Modernized)

**Release date:** 2026-11-09
**Tag:** `v1.1.9.2-modernized-beta`
**Baseline:** `v1.1.9.1-modernized` (`81fe90b`)
**Package:** `Carnivores2_MEE_v1.1.9.2_Modernized.zip`

> **This is a beta pre-release.** It is built and tested locally, but it has not
> been fully hand-validated. Expect rough edges, keep backups of `config.cfg`,
> `trophy*.sav`/`.sab` and your `HUNTDAT`, and report reproducible problems.
> It is not a ModDB release and carries no ModDB label.

This build contains the changes from the 256 commits after `v1.1.9.1`,
including the live modder workbench, dynamic character roster, audio/collision
fixes, shop and night-lighting features, and a large safety/refactor pass.

## Water sounds and footsteps

This beta fixes the terrain-related water-sound problems:

- **Dry decks and objects above water now use land footsteps.** The old
  `ONWATER` test only checked whether the tile was flooded, so walking on a
  bridge, causeway or other object above a river played the water footstep set
  and spawned ripple circles on the water surface below. The check now also
  requires the player's feet to be at or below the water surface (`678352a`).
  Wading in shallow water and surface swimming keep their water footsteps.
- **Dry-land footsteps no longer become water splashes after long uptime.**
  After roughly 24.9 days of Windows uptime, `timeGetTime()` becomes negative
  and `RealTime % 3` indexed before the dry-step array, selecting loaded water
  impact samples on dry terrain. Landing, dry and wet footsteps now use one
  unsigned, bounded sample index (`1593709`).
- **Level reloads cannot return the previous level's cached sound.** OpenAL
  buffers are now keyed by sample pointer, length and a resource generation,
  and retired buffers are swept only after their voices/ambient sources release
  them (`308ac66`). This removes a separate route to wrong water/ambient audio
  after leaving and re-entering a level.

## Highlights

### Live modder workbench

- Native Dear ImGui F10 inspector for the OpenGL renderer, replacing the legacy
  GL F10 panel: Fog/Waves/Sun, Night, Beam, Water and Audio groups.
- Validated sliders and exact entry, loaded-baseline reset/compare, grouped
  undo/redo, help text and change review.
- Water tab exposes `water_mipmap` and `water_wave_fade_rings`.
- Audio panel exposes 32 reverb fields across banks 0-6/8; environment 7 stays
  dry/read-only.
- Collision inspector with a bounded nearby-placement list, loaded `ofBOUND`
  copy, selected/resolved floor/ceiling contributions and a default-off X-ray.
- Optional config persistence: changed, recognized keys can be written through
  a byte-preserving merge with a numbered backup and atomic replace. Values
  apply on the next launch.
- F8 hands input to/from the hunt; Escape returns from Test to UI. Opening the
  panel does **not** pause the simulation.
- The software renderer keeps its legacy panel; the native workbench is
  OpenGL-only.

### Dynamic character roster

- Character definitions are no longer limited to a fixed 128-entry array.
  Storage is sized from the `characters` entries in `_RES.TXT`; model records
  are frozen before placement so live character pointers stay valid.
- Spawn links and trophy-group membership grow as needed, while live-character,
  AI-family, trophy-display, pack-member and per-definition spawn-info limits
  remain independent and enforced.
- Saved trophy `.sav`/`.sab` layouts are unchanged. Definition IDs remain
  script-order IDs, so append variants rather than inserting/reordering entries
  if old saves must keep referring to the same animals.
- On x86 the metadata budget allows up to 1,833 definitions; 1,024 is within
  that budget. This is not a promise that a 1,024+ roster can spawn every
  definition in one hunt: the 1,024-entry pack-type table, 16 MiB `_RES.TXT`
  input budget, 256 live-character slots, 128 AI families, 128 trophy display
  slots and the menu's 32-bit launch flags still apply. Regression coverage
  currently reaches 513 definitions.

See `docs/MODDING-ROSTER.md` for authoring and capacity details.

### Shop, night lighting and flashlight

- Shop externalization: named prices plus icon/text redirects for existing
  equipment rows, with a read-only shop checker. Legacy positional prices still
  work, and dynamic shop rows are not implemented.
- Moonlit night lighting with coordinated sky/fog grading and a Classic
  fallback. The flashlight restores brightness and color inside its beam, and
  night vision wins when both are active.
- The flashlight is a seventh hunt-screen item with its own price/fallback and
  configurable key/cone/range.

## Fixed

- **Bridge/deck and dry-terrain water footsteps** — see the water-sounds section
  above.
- **Death camera and full-roster safety:** the hunter-death body append is
  guarded at live-character capacity, callers receive the appended body, and a
  body-independent camera fallback prevents indexing past `Characters[]`.
- **Trophy pickup queue:** bounded at the real array capacity; a full queue drops
  the extra pickup request and its acknowledgement sound while still recording
  the trophy.
- **Pack/killer/demo pointers:** rebuilt after character removal; stale pickup
  entries are dropped, and leader-follow uses a bounded accessor.
- **Overlapping array compaction:** bullets, particles, blood trails and ambient
  sounds use `memmove` and active counts instead of overlapping `memcpy` and
  fixed over-copies.
- **Empty/oversized spawn regions and idle groups:** empty lists fall back to the
  default map region; zero or over-capacity groups are rejected before indexing.
- **MSVC `/analyze` findings:** caps-lock HUD gate, weapon morph phase, fish
  range defaults, trophy-save checked reads, empty weapon selection, Win32
  result guards, `ScriptFieldFail` noreturn and initialized flag-guarded locals.
- Trophy-room view distance now follows the profile setting; trophy exhibits are
  inert to bullets.
- Weapon viewmodel fog is applied once, removing the pocket-fog specular seam.
- Generic fish use their authored 1,024-unit wander radius instead of the
  Mosasaurus radius.
- Two-digit trophy names at profile 10+, strict 16-bit engine TGA validation,
  case-insensitive menu config keys, legacy OpenAL driver mappings 4-5, and the
  F10 PgDn direction.
- Menu/parser robustness: missing-file names, incomplete-area refusal, clean
  session-exit logging, checked model-loader reads, empty weapon selection, and
  the unused Q exit-countdown branch removal.

## Added and changed

- **Data-driven workbench and config tooling:** the 59-descriptor tuning registry,
  workbench save review, config merge/persistence, and the `tools/perf` capture
  and measurement harness.
- **New config keys** include the workbench-backed water keys; existing keys and
  legacy positional data remain supported. Workbench-written values apply on the
  next launch.
- **Modding:** per-species `agressMulti` and the optional `ai_logging` trace were
  already in v1.1.9.1; they remain documented in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Internal quality:** `ScriptParser.cpp` split into five translation units;
  menu input/drawing/config/profile IO split out; dead-code/comment/global
  sweeps; test suite consolidated from 686 to 487 cases while keeping contract
  assertions; per-preset MSVC warning baselines and OpenCppCoverage CI.
- **Collision:** tilted-slab underside/headroom fitting and opt-in oriented
  `ofBOUND` box fitting; `boundbox_fit 0` remains the legacy default and
  `boundbox_fit 1` enables oriented fitting at any baked angle.

## Known beta limitations

- **RoundUp total conversion:** the modern menu does not yet read RoundUp's
  `_RES.TXT areatable`, has no `characters` block in its `_MENU.TXT`, and has no
  `.c2map` files. Its HUNT screen therefore has no areas/dinosaurs and cannot
  launch; the accessory/options layout also predates the newer rows. Use the
  mod's original launcher with this beta. The investigation is recorded in
  `CarnivoresDoc/working/sessions/2026-11-08-roundup-menu-broken-state-catalogue.md`.
- **Workbench acceptance:** the water-tab and save-path owner checks, audio
  listening, real-map collision-inspector retest and matched game-overhead
  measurement remain open.
- **Dynamic roster:** reporter/Genesis Redux assets, foreground owner play,
  software-renderer gameplay, high-ID trophy/survival gameplay, allocation/fault
  injection and a full audit of every remaining data limit are still open. The
  highest automated roster fixture is 513 definitions.
- **No exhaustive manual matrix** was run for this beta; the automated five-preset
  matrix and warning/release-flag gates are green, but no game/menu launch was
  performed while packaging.
- **Tuning packs** are specified but not implemented; `config.cfg` remains the
  supported persistence path. **x64/GL + x86/Soft split** is deferred. The
  unmerged `private/mee-1.2` compatibility commit is not part of this package.
- The legacy workbench fields (Night/Beam/Water/Audio) are runtime-only except
  the recognized keys exposed through the save path; the legacy thirteen and RGB
  tint remain dump/runtime-only.

## Upgrade notes

1. Back up `config.cfg`, `trophy*.sav`, `trophy*.sab` and your `HUNTDAT` before
   replacing files.
2. Copy the package contents next to your existing `HUNTDAT`; keep your personal
   `config.cfg` if you do not want the generated defaults.
3. The dynamic roster preserves definition IDs. Append new variants at the end;
   do not reorder existing definitions if saves must retain their meaning.
4. Workbench saves create numbered `.before-workbench.bak` snapshots and apply on
   the next launch. A read-only or stale destination is refused rather than
   partially written.
5. RoundUp users should keep using that mod's original launcher until its menu
   data/layout compatibility is implemented.
