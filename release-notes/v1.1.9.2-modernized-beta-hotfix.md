# Carnivores 2 MEE v1.1.9.2 Beta Hotfix (Modernized)

**Release date:** 2026-11-14  
**Tag:** `v1.1.9.2-modernized-beta-hotfix`  
**Baseline:** `v1.1.9.2-modernized-beta`  
**Package:** `Carnivores2_MEE_v1.1.9.2_Hotfix_Modernized.zip`

This is a **beta prerelease hotfix**, not a new stable release or ModDB release.
It includes the earlier beta's features and the fixes below.

## Fixes

- **Trophy room and unarmed observer sessions:** fix the erroneous
  `No weapon is available for this hunt (empty weapon selection)` termination.
  Intentional `wep=0` sessions keep their weapon hidden and use a safe metadata
  index. Ordinary hunts still require a weapon. The owner confirmed trophy-room
  entry works after updating the software renderer.
- **Ambient audio after level reloads:** ambient and moving-ambient channels
  now rebind when the resource generation changes, even if a new sound reuses
  the old sample's address and length.
- **Authored reversed spawn bounds:** preserve the legacy calculation and RNG
  sequence for working reversed regions, including the stock Area 7 Poacher
  Trap. Guard undefined arithmetic without collapsing all reversed spans.
- **Larger character rosters:** double the metadata budget from 64 to 128 MiB.
  x86 Release/Shipping accepts **3,667 definitions** instead of 1,833; the next
  entry is rejected safely. Memory still grows with loaded data, not the budget.

## Important capacity limits

The higher roster budget does **not** increase the independent 1,024 pack-type
limit, 256 live-character slots, 32 pack members, AI-family table, trophy display
slots, menu launch flags or 16 MiB `_RES.TXT` input budget. A mod where every
definition generates a pack can hit the pack-type limit first. Loaded model,
texture, animation and sound payloads also share the 32-bit address space.

Full-ceiling automated tests cover parsing, model-record finalization and
rejection beyond the metadata budget. Asset-backed large-roster gameplay
remains tested to **513 definitions**, not 3,667. High-ID trophy save/re-entry,
survival, broad mod compatibility and exhaustive manual gameplay remain open.

See `docs/MODDING-ROSTER.md` in the source repository for details. The earlier
beta's RoundUp menu compatibility and workbench acceptance caveats still apply;
see the [original beta release](https://github.com/Tibbee/carnivores2-mee-modernized/releases/tag/v1.1.9.2-modernized-beta).

## Installation / upgrading

1. Back up `config.cfg`, `trophy*.sav`, `trophy*.sab` and your game data.
2. Copy the contents of the extracted package folder next to your existing
   `HUNTDAT`. Keep your existing `config.cfg` to preserve personal settings.
3. Replace **all three executables**: `Carnivores2Menu.exe`, `v_gl.ren` and
   **`v_soft.ren`**. Updating only the menu or OpenGL does not fix software mode.
4. Launch `Carnivores2Menu.exe`.

Saved trophy layouts and script-order definition IDs are unchanged. Do not
reorder definitions if old saves must retain their meaning.
